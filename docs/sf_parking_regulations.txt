SAN FRANCISCO MUNICIPAL TRANSPORTATION AGENCY (SFMTA) - OFFICIAL PARKING RULES & REGULATIONS

1. CURB COLOR DESIGNATIONS AND RESTRICTIONS
- RED CURB: No stopping, standing, or parking at any time under any circumstances. Used for bus zones, fire hydrants, curb returns, and safety visibility clearance at intersections. Violators are cited ($110+) and subject to immediate tow.
- YELLOW CURB: Active commercial vehicle loading and unloading only, typically active Monday through Saturday 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM unless signs specify otherwise. Non-commercial vehicles may stop for up to 3 minutes only while actively loading or unloading passengers. Vehicles parked without active loading are subject to citation and tow.
- WHITE CURB: Passenger loading zone only, 5-minute maximum limit. Active during business hours of adjacent establishments, theaters, schools, or hotels. Driver must remain with the vehicle or within immediate proximity.
- GREEN CURB: Short-term parking zone, typically 10-minute or 15-minute maximum limit. Enforced Monday through Saturday 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM unless signs indicate otherwise. Vehicles exceeding the posted time limit receive parking citations.
- BLUE CURB: Reserved exclusively for vehicles displaying a valid California Disabled Person (DP) placard, DP license plates, or Disabled Veteran (DV) plates. No time limits apply unless posted, and meter fees are waived. Unauthorized parking is heavily penalized (minimum $450 fine plus tow fees).

2. GENERAL ON-STREET CITYWIDE PARKING RESTRICTIONS
- 72-HOUR MAXIMUM LIMIT: Vehicles cannot remain parked in the same spot on any public street for more than 72 consecutive hours without moving at least one-tenth (1/10) of a mile (approx. 500 feet). Odometers and tire markings are tracked; abandoned or stagnant vehicles will be tagged with a 24-hour warning notice and subsequently towed.
- WHEELS CURBED ON HILLS: Whenever parking on any grade of 3 percent or more in San Francisco, drivers MUST curb their front wheels:
  * Facing Uphill: Turn front wheels AWAY from the curb and let the vehicle roll back gently until the rear of the front tire touches the curb.
  * Facing Downhill: Turn front wheels TOWARD the curb and let the vehicle roll forward gently until the front tire touches the curb.
  * No Curb Present: Turn wheels toward the side of the road so the vehicle would roll off the road away from traffic.
  * Failure to curb wheels on any street with grade >= 3% carries an automatic citation ($68+).
- DRIVEWAY CLEARANCE: Vehicles may not block any public or private driveway. In San Francisco, drivers must leave at least 2 feet of clearance between their vehicle bumpers and the outer edge of any driveway curb cut or slope. Homeowners may park in front of their own single-family residential driveway provided the vehicle is registered to that address and does not extend into the sidewalk pathway.
- SIDEWALK PARKING: It is strictly prohibited to park any vehicle on a sidewalk, crosswalk, or over a pedestrian curb ramp, even in residential areas or outside private garages. Sidewalks must maintain a clear 4-foot pedestrian path at all times.

3. STREET CLEANING & TOW-AWAY ZONES
- STREET SWEEPING: Street cleaning schedules are strictly enforced across all residential and commercial districts. Sweeping periods typically last 2 hours (e.g., 8:00 AM - 10:00 AM on 1st and 3rd Wednesdays). Parking during posted street cleaning hours results in immediate citation ($85+), even if the mechanical sweeper has already passed by.
- COMMUTE RUSH-HOUR TOW-AWAY LANES: Major thoroughfares (e.g., Bush St, Pine St, Geary Blvd, 4th St, Mission St) convert curb parking lanes into active traffic lanes during peak commute hours (typically 7:00 AM - 9:00 AM inbound and 3:00 PM - 7:00 PM outbound). Parking in these lanes during restricted hours results in immediate tow without prior warning. Typical tow release fee exceeds $500.
- TEMPORARY NO-PARKING ZONES: Yellow and white cardboard signs posted on utility poles for construction, moving trucks, or street fairs must be posted at least 72 hours in advance. Always inspect nearby poles and trees for posted temporary restrictions before leaving your vehicle.

4. RESIDENTIAL PERMIT PARKING (RPP)
- RPP AREAS: San Francisco designates residential neighborhoods with lettered zones (e.g., Area A through Area Z).
- Non-Permit Holders: Drivers without an active RPP permit for that specific zone are restricted to a maximum 2-hour stay during enforcement hours (typically Monday through Friday or Saturday, 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM or 9:00 PM depending on the neighborhood).
- Permit Holders: Vehicles displaying an active sticker permit or digital permit for that specific zone are exempt from the 2-hour restriction and can park up to 72 hours continuously.
- Moving within the Zone: To reset the 2-hour clock, non-permit vehicles must be moved at least one block away (at least 500 feet). Moving the car across the street on the same block does NOT reset the timer.

5. PARKING METERS & PAYMENT RULES
- Operating Hours: Most meters operate Monday through Saturday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. High-demand areas, Fisherman's Wharf, Downtown/SOMA, and areas near Chase Center and Oracle Park operate meters until 10:00 PM and on Sundays (12:00 PM to 6:00 PM).
- Demand-Responsive Meter Rates: Meter rates vary dynamically by neighborhood, block, and time of day (ranging from $0.50/hr to $8.00+/hr, with special surge pricing up to $12.00+/hr during Chase Center or Oracle Park major events).
- Payment Options: Meters accept credit cards, coins, and mobile payment via PayByPhone.
- Broken Meters: Parking at a broken or defective meter is permitted only up to the maximum posted time limit (e.g., 1 hour or 2 hours). Drivers cannot park all day at broken meters.
